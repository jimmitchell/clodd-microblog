{{- /* A post's rendered body, with u-photo added to every when the post is a photo post. Microformats parsers read a photo post's type from its u-photo, and here the pictures live in the body (Micropub and Micro.blog both put them there) rather than in a field of their own. Arguments: page, kind. */ -}} {{- $html := string .page.Content -}} {{- if eq .kind "photo" -}} {{- range uniq (findRE `]*>` $html) -}} {{- $tag := . -}} {{- $new := "" -}} {{- if findRE `\sclass="` $tag 1 -}} {{- $new = replaceRE `(\sclass=")` "${1}u-photo " $tag 1 -}} {{- else -}} {{- $new = replaceRE `^
